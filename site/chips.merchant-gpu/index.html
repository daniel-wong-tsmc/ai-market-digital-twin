Demand for AI accelerators runs far ahead of what packaging and memory can deliver, and the single thing capping shipments is advanced packaging and high-bandwidth memory that stay sold out into 2027. The question that moves the next rating is whether your packaging ramp and new memory capacity close the supply gap faster than orders grow, and we sit more cautious than the crowd because custom in-house chips are taking share from merchant GPUs faster than headline share figures suggest. The clearest early sign of change would be packaging waits falling below a year, which would show first in your own booking calendar and quoted lead times.
