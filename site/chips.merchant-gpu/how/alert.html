How the alert color was decided

Today: ORANGE (before: GREEN)

The ladder (first match from the top wins)

  • GREEN: nothing on the watchlist moved this week
  • YELLOW: one thing moved: the gap band changed, a high-confidence call moved, the main limiting factor changed, or two calls moved together
  • ORANGE: two yellow-level things happened at once, a high-confidence call broke, or demand worsened while the gap moved toward glut
  • RED: a confirmed structural break: a high-confidence call broke AND the gap band flipped in the same week

What fired this run

  • the demand-vs-supply gap band changed within the last week
  • the main limiting factor changed within the last week
  • a high-confidence call moved within the last week
  • two or more calls moved in the same direction within the last week
